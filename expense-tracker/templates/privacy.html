Privacy & Security

Privacy Policy

Your privacy is fundamental to how Spendly is designed. We believe your personal financial data should remain strictly yours.

Effective date: March 2026 · Version 1.0
01

Data We Collect

We only collect information directly necessary to provide you with personal expense tracking services:

  • Account Information: Your name, email address, and securely hashed passwords when you register.
  • Expense & Transaction Records: Transaction amounts, dates, expense categories, payment methods, and optional personal notes you choose to log.
  • Technical Usage: Minimal session data and cookie identifiers strictly necessary to keep you authenticated.
02

How We Use Your Data

We use the data collected strictly to operate, maintain, and improve your personal finance dashboard:

  • To calculate your category breakdowns, spending trends, and monthly summaries.
  • To authenticate your sign-in sessions and protect account access.
  • To allow you to create, edit, filter, and delete your expense history.

◈ Zero data selling: We never sell, lease, or distribute your spending habits or personal details to advertisers, data brokers, or marketing networks.

03

Data Storage

Your records are stored securely in dedicated database environments. All sensitive credentials, such as account passwords, are protected using industry-standard one-way cryptographic hashing algorithms.

You retain full control over your records. When you delete an expense or delete your account, your data is promptly and permanently removed from our active databases.

04

Third Party Services

Spendly operates as a focused, self-contained application:

  • We do not embed third-party tracking pixels, invasive analytics beacons, or advertising behavioral scripts.
  • We only utilize core infrastructure providers (such as hosting and database services) necessary to serve the web application reliably.
05

Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or how your personal financial data is handled, feel free to contact us:

  • Email: privacy@spendly.app
  • Website: spendly.local
